WHAT THIS SETUP DOES
ArtFlawless Studio is a free convenience interface for an image-generation workflow that runs in ComfyUI on your own computer.
During setup, ComfyUI, its add-ons and the AI models (about 25 GB in total) are downloaded from their original sources.
Nothing is generated or sent anywhere: all images are created on your computer.
ArtFlawless Studio is an independent interface, not affiliated with Comfy Org or the ComfyUI developers. ComfyUI is free software under the GNU GPL v3.0 and is installed unmodified.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
Minimum:
 - Windows 10 64-bit (version 1803 or newer) or Windows 11
 - NVIDIA GeForce RTX graphics card with 8 GB video memory (for example RTX 2070, 3060 Ti, 4060), up-to-date NVIDIA driver
 - 32 GB RAM, 64-bit processor with 4 cores
 - 50 GB free disk space (SSD), internet connection for the setup and the model downloads
 - Microsoft Edge (included in Windows 10 and 11); it also provides the WebView2 engine used by the Studio window
Recommended:
 - Windows 11, or Windows 10 22H2
 - NVIDIA RTX card with 12 GB or more video memory (for example RTX 3060 12 GB, 4070 or better)
 - 64 GB RAM, processor with 6 or more cores
 - 100 GB free space on an NVMe SSD (room for all three model families)
Tested on: RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB, 64 GB RAM, Windows 10 22H2. AMD and Intel graphics cards are not supported.
